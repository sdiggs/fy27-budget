Four Ways to Read the FY2027 Budget

Explore the President's 2027 request — by agency, by program, and by what grows or gets cut.

Circles are sized by dollars.

Each panel is an agency, sized by its 2027 request. The circles inside are the programs the budget names, scaled within each agency and colored green for increases, red for cuts.

How to read it. Color follows the budget's own framing: green where a line goes up, red where it comes down. All Programs floats every named increase to the top and sinks every cut to the bottom, each circle sized by its dollar change. Agency Totals sizes each agency by its 2027 base discretionary request and colors it by percent change from 2026. By Department nests the named programs inside their agency. Changes places each agency by its percent change.

Honest limits. This summary volume gives agency toplines cleanly (Table S-3) but only the program changes its authors chose to highlight — not the full account-level tree, which lives in the Appendix and Analytical Perspectives volumes. A few figures are funding levels, multi-year financing, or mandatory funds rather than annual deltas; those are flagged in their tooltips. "Budget cites" text paraphrases the document's stated rationale, not an endorsement. Figures in $ millions unless noted. Source: OMB, Budget of the U.S. Government, FY2027. Form after the NYT's Four Ways to Slice Obama's 2013 Budget Proposal.
